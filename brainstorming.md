## New App Ideas - Evaluate and Categorize (We need three for the assignment, anyone can fill in the other stuff)

1. Runner's Group App
   - **Description**: Social media meets running, can join groups of runners and see them live and save the run for later so you can view the path/course, can compete for leaderboards stats and more.
   - **Category:** Social Media, Fitness
   - **Mobile:** Mobile is essential since people need to carry their phone to track the run and see the time as well as being able to join their group/friends live.
   - **Story:** Connects runners of all levels to create a real-time running club. It turns running from a solitary task into a motivating, and interactive social experience where no one has to run alone.
   - **Market:** Casual joggers, marathon trainees, run club organizers, and fitness influencers.
   - **Habit:** Users open it every time they tie their laces for a run. Throughout the week, they check it to view friends' routes, compare leaderboard standings, and schedule upcoming group runs.
   - **Scope:** 
        - **V1**: Basic GPS run tracking for individuals, route saving, and a local leaderboard. Could be tested by a small group of friends.
        - **V2**: Real-time group creation, live map tracking (seeing where your friends are on the map during the run), and post-run group chat.
        - **V3**: Advanced social features like photo sharing on routes, audio cues ("Your friend John just passed you!"), and public discoverable local run clubs.
        - **V4**: Wearable device integration (Apple Watch, Garmin) and virtual challenges system

2. Flashcard Studying App
- **Description**: A focused flashcard app with study scheduling, progress tracking, a cram mode for last-minute sessions
- **Category**: Education
- **Mobile**: Being mobile oriented is important since it provides convenience for studying on the go by matching the format of traditional flashcards.
- **Story**: Studying in a way that fits you is already difficult. Current systems like Quizlet and Anki are at times too complicated and too simple. This app aims to bridge the gap by providing a convenient and flexible platform to build decks, maintain study habits with a calendar, and shift into cram mode when time is limited.
- **Market**: High school and college students, self-learners
- **Habit**: Users open the app daily to maintain a study streak. Users may also set certain intervals of study throughout a week.
- **Scope**:
    - V1: Create and manage decks, basic flashcard study flow with flip animation, and a calendar heatmap showing days studied.
    - V2: Progress tracking per deck. This will display how many cards are in "learning", "review", and "mastered" states. Cram mode that shuffles the full deck on repeat.
    - V3: Import and export system for flashcards for sharing decks between users and collaborative deck building.
    - V4: Streak rewards and study milestones, such as badges for hitting daily streaks, completing a full deck, or hitting a mastery threshold

3. Workout Tracking App
- **Description**: An app that lets users look up workout routines, save workouts, and track their exercise progress over time.
- **Category**: Health and Fitness
- **Mobile**: This works well on mobile because users may want to check workouts while they are at the gym or exercising. Having routines and saved workouts on a phone is convenient and fits the way people normally use fitness apps.
- **Story**: The app has a clear purpose because many people want a simple way to organize workouts and stay consistent with exercise. It is easy to understand and has practical value for everyday use.
- **Market**: Gym-goers, beginners, athletes, and anyone interested in fitness.
- **Habit**: Users could open it several times a week or even daily depending on how often they work out. It has good repeat use because exercise usually happens on a regular schedule.
- **Scope**:
    - V1: Search for workouts, save routines, and view a basic workout list.
    - V2: Track completed workouts and organize routines by muscle group or workout type.
    - V3: Add progress tracking such as workout history or personal records.
    - V4: Add reminders, streaks, or more detailed fitness stats.

## Final App Idea Chosen

**Flashcard Studying App**

Our group chose the Flashcard Studying App as the final idea for the project. We picked it because it has a clear purpose, fits the mobile format well, and gives us enough features and screens to build a complete app. It can be useful for students who want a simple way to create decks, study cards, and keep track of their progress. The idea also has room to grow over time with extra features while still being well-defined for an initial version.
